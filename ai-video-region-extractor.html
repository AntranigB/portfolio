This was a creature comfort: I did not want to open DaVinci Resolve every time I just needed to crop a recording.

What it does

A small macOS utility lets me save named rectangular regions, choose a recording, preview a crop, and export it. FFmpeg re-encodes the cropped video while copying audio tracks unchanged. It checks the recording dimensions against the saved layout, preserves the original, and refuses to overwrite an existing export. It also supports command-line use.

How it was built

Vibe-coded project: I supplied the idea, requirements, and feedback; AI wrote the implementation.

Technology: Bash, AppleScript dialogs, JSON configuration, FFmpeg and FFprobe; Apple hardware H.264 encoding when available.

Completed for the intended crop-and-export workflow.
